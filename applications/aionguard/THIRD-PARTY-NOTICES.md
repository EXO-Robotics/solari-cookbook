# Third-party licenses and notices

The AionGuard license does not replace third-party licenses, copyrights or notices.

- [Imported project document notice](LICENSES/Imported-project-documents-notice.txt)
- [Pinetree Research upstream notice](LICENSES/MIT-Pinetree-Research.txt)
- [Dependency inventory](LICENSES/third-party/inventory.csv)
- [Exact license and NOTICE snapshots](LICENSES/third-party/snapshot-manifest.json)
- [Presentation asset provenance](docs/assets/README.md)

The dependency inventory covers locked packages. The copied notices are evidence, not a complete notice bundle for every binary or installer. Actual distributions must also account for bundled dependencies, missing package notices, Chromium/guest images, fonts and media. Provider service terms are separate from SDK licenses. Separately licensed source remains under its own terms; the application license applies only to rights the licensors control.
