The adoption gate conservatively requires rights-holder approval for every covered contribution.

## Future contributors

Current CONTRIBUTING preserves MIT for baseline components and requires explicit written agreement before accepting contributions to identified source-available components. Before accepting contributions into a newly restricted component, present an attorney-approved inbound agreement with clear outbound terms and commercial sublicensing authority. Merely submitting a pull request or adding a DCO-style authorship statement does not automatically provide that authority. Keep separate contribution routes for MIT-only files. Do not retroactively bind past contributors, assert all-rights assignment, or require patent rights without review.
