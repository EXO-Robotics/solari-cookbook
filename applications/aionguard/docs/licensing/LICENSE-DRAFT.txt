No code or version is covered by this draft; the review manifest is empty.

Previously MIT-licensed material, Pinetree Research/Solari upstream code,
dependencies, and separately licensed assets are excluded. If a file contains
both MIT baseline material and eligible new additions, these terms apply
only to the identified additions. Earlier valid permissions remain usable.
These terms do not override a recipient's independent MIT or other license
for the same material. Notices and the coverage map must make this clear.

deployment solely on the organization's behalf. Ordinary use of inspection

Gratis redistribution may include source or executable forms and modifications,

3. Proposed commercial boundary

"Commercial Exploitation" means:
(a) selling, reselling or distributing Covered Material or a derivative
containing it for compensation;
(b) hosting Covered Material to provide functionality to paying external
customers, whether the charge is for it separately or for a larger service;
or (c) including Covered Material or a derivative containing it as part of
a product or subscription offered commercially for compensation, including
a client provided without a separate charge as part of that paid offering.

Commercial Exploitation would require a separate written paid commercial
agreement signed by the licensee and the authorized Licensors before the
activity begins. Fees or revenue sharing, if agreed, are set in that separate
agreement. This draft sets no royalty, default invoice, retroactive payment,
or right to revenue from unrelated business activity. The written agreement
must identify the material and permitted activity; it cannot sell rights
belonging to an unconsenting contributor or third party.

Ad-supported services, donations/cost recovery, freemium trials, affiliates,
and paid implementation/support without software resale or paid hosting
remain drafting questions. Counsel and contributors must settle those
boundaries expressly in the final terms, including precedence between paid
managed hosting and the contractor/internal-deployment exception; this draft
does not silently
classify them or impose fees for them.
