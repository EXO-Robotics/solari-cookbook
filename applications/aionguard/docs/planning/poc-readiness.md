## First in-event gate

Build in a new repository only after the organizer’s permitted start. Verify the intended Vercel team/project and account quota, prepare the clean pinned browser image, and prove the exact Mail-origin route through held navigation, fresh Vercel inspection, real CLI Astra, broker/evaluator and case snapshot. Repeat from two fresh attempts; preserve all failures and elapsed time. Generic probes do not substitute for this gate. The roughly 90-minute M1 checkpoint is a scope/capacity review, not a guarantee or automatic expansion permission.

Keep the original four-credit investigation, Phase 2-before-Phase 3 order, and final-hour rehearsal reserve. Phase 2 requires its entire wrong/missing secret, unknown/altered ID, expired/revoked pair, delayed delivery, monitor failure, deduplication and fresh-repeat acceptance table in the actual product. Reserve at least 30 minutes of existing Phase 2 allowance for those negative controls; do not reduce the gate to the happy path.

Continuity remains between jobs: revoke old submission authority, independently confirm no accepted/running work remains, then admit a distinct scoped replacement. Unknown/nonempty state waits. No in-flight cancellation, general leases, state migration or extra exposure scenario is added.
